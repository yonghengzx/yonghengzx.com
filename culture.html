Compliance & Responsibility

Global compliance, treated as standard practice

We trade across borders, so documentation, sourcing and packaging controls are written into the order process rather than handled at the end of it.

01

Export compliance

Every export consignment is accompanied by the required customs documentation, and we check restricted-party and sanctioned-territory status before a shipment is accepted.

02

Responsible sourcing

We confirm manufacturing origin and material claims with the factory before goods are listed, and we decline suppliers who will not answer that audit.

03

Packaging responsibility

Packaging is specified for reuse and safe disposal, and the cost of compliant materials is carried in the quotation rather than passed on as a surprise.
