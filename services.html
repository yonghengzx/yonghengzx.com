How We Work

A four-stage engagement

The same sequence applies whether the request is a single wholesale consignment or a full brand promotion and event programme.

Consultation & scoping

We confirm the market, the product category, the volume and the deadline, and identify which of the eight business lines the request falls under.

Sourcing & brand alignment

Suppliers are selected against your specification, samples are approved, and brand promotion is planned wherever the product line calls for it.

Fulfilment, promotion & event delivery

Import and export documentation, wholesale and retail fulfilment, campaign rollout and event delivery are executed as one coordinated workstream.

Ongoing management in the app

Orders, inventory, supply chain and reporting move into the mobile management app, where the account keeps full visibility of every business line.
