1. Introduction and scope

Endlesshine Co., Limited ("Endlesshine", "we", "us") is a Hong Kong company whose registered office is at ROOM 1602, 16/F, WING ON CENTRE, 111 CONNAUGHT ROAD CENTRAL, HONG KONG. Our business scope is "Import, export, wholesale and retail of cultural and creative gifts and daily necessities; brand promotion, business information consultation and event planning, excluding financial, securities and insurance business".

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, transfer and protect personal data in connection with two channels:

  • the corporate website published at yonghengzx.com (the "Website"), including the pages, enquiry forms and corporate content made available there; and
  • the mobile management application distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play (the "App"), which provides order, inventory, supply chain and consulting functions for our business contacts.

It applies to visitors to the Website, registered users of the App, business contacts who correspond with us, and mobile app users. Where we refer to "personal data", we mean information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person. Where we refer to "you", we mean the individual concerned.

We act as the controller of personal data that we determine the purposes and means of processing for. Certain advertising, analytics and payment providers act as independent controllers or as our processors in respect of specific data sets, as explained in sections 5 and 9.

2. Information we collect

We collect personal data directly from you, automatically through the operation of the Website and the App, and, in limited circumstances, from third parties. The categories below describe the data we handle.

2.1 Account data

When you create an account in the App or open a business relationship with us, we collect:

  • your name;
  • your business email address;
  • your company name and trading details; and
  • your role or position within that company.

Credentials such as passwords are processed by the App in hashed form. We never receive or store your plaintext password.

2.2 Device and technical data

To keep the App and the Website secure and functioning, we automatically collect device and technical data, including device model, operating system and its version, App version, IP address, network and carrier information, and crash logs generated when the App encounters an error.

2.3 Usage data

We record the pages you visit on the Website, the features you use in the App, and the in-app events you trigger, including order events, inventory lookups and dashboard views. This information helps us operate the product, improve performance and understand which features are used.

2.4 Optional precise location

Where you grant the operating system permission, the App may collect your precise location. We use this only to support visits to our wholesale showroom and to confirm attendance at trade events. We do not access location data if permission is declined, and declining permission does not affect the remaining functionality of the App.

2.5 Advertising identifiers

We and our advertising partners may access the device advertising identifier assigned by your operating system, including the Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) and the Google Advertising ID (AAID), together with any identifier reset to the platform default or assigned by the device. Section 9 explains how these identifiers are used and how to limit them.

2.6 Information submitted through enquiry forms

When you submit an enquiry through the Website or the App, we collect the name, business email address, company and message content you provide. We use this information to respond to you and to maintain a record of the enquiry.

3. How we use information

We use personal data only for the purposes set out below. Each category of information collected under section 2 is used for the purposes stated here.

  • Operating the App and the Website. We use account data, device data and usage data to authenticate users, present screens, maintain sessions, deliver updates and diagnose faults.
  • Order and inventory management. We use account data, enquiry data and usage data to record orders, allocate stock, prepare shipments and maintain inventory positions across our wholesale and retail operations.
  • Supply chain tracking. We use account data, order data and usage data to track consignments through import and export workflows, including documents passed to logistics and freight partners.
  • Consulting dashboards. We use account data and usage data to populate business information consultation and event planning dashboards, including performance indicators and inventory or order summaries visible to you within the App.
  • Brand promotion and event planning delivery. We use account data and enquiry data to plan and deliver brand promotion campaigns and events, to confirm arrangements with business partners, and to maintain the associated records.
  • Customer support. We use enquiry data, account data and technical data to investigate and resolve support requests, and to maintain a correspondence record.
  • Personalised advertising. We use advertising identifiers, usage data and inferred interests to select advertising that is more relevant to you, subject to your consent and opt-out rights as described in section 9.
  • Analytics. We use aggregated usage and technical data to measure traffic, app adoption, feature engagement and error rates, and to plan capacity.
  • Security and fraud prevention. We use technical data, usage data and IP addresses to detect abuse, prevent fraudulent transactions, secure our systems and investigate incidents.
  • Legal compliance. We use account data, transaction records and correspondence to meet accounting, tax, customs, export control and other statutory obligations, and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We do not sell personal data for money. We do not use the content of your messages or documents for advertising purposes.

9. Advertising and monetisation

This section describes our advertising practices in detail, because advertising involves the most extensive processing of personal data on our services.

9.1 Advertising SDKs and ad formats

The App integrates third-party advertising software development kits. When an ad is requested, the relevant SDK may collect the device advertising identifier, IP address, device model, operating system and version, App version, coarse location derived from the IP address, language, and information about ad interactions, such as impressions, clicks and completions. The App may serve the following advertising formats:

  • app-open (splash) advertising, displayed when the App is launched;
  • rewarded video advertising, displayed in exchange for a reward;
  • interstitial advertising, displayed at natural breaks between content; and
  • banner advertising, displayed as a persistent strip within a screen.

Section 10 describes each format, why it is shown and the controls available to you.

9.2 Personalised and non-personalised advertising

Advertising delivered through the App and the Website may be personalised or non-personalised. Personalised advertising uses your advertising identifier, together with information about your usage and, where consent has been given, inferred interests, to select and to measure advertising addressed to you. Non-personalised, or contextual, advertising is selected by reference to the content of the page or the context of the App screen, without reference to your advertising identifier or to any profile of you.

Where consent is required, we present a consent banner or an in-app consent prompt before personalised advertising is served. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, consent is requested under the GDPR and the UK GDPR, and refusal results in non-personalised advertising being served instead. In the United States, subject to the state privacy laws listed in section 4 and to the California CCPA/CPRA, you may opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and you may exercise that right through the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link available in our Website footer and in the App's privacy settings, or by enabling Global Privacy Control as described in section 15.

On Apple devices we honour the App Tracking Transparency framework. The App presents the system permission prompt and does not request tracking, or enable personalised advertising SDK behaviour, unless you have granted permission. On Android devices we observe the advertising identifier permission prompt, and where you decline, the App operates with contextual advertising and non-personalised identifiers.

9.3 Your control over advertising identifiers

You can reset or limit the advertising identifier at operating system level, which applies to all applications on your device. On iOS, this is the "Allow Apps to Request to Track" setting. On Android, this is "Delete advertising ID" and the "Opt out of ad personalisation" setting. Resetting or limiting the identifier reduces the personalisation available to advertising partners, and may reduce the number of ads you see. You may also opt out within the App's privacy settings, or via the applicable opt-out mechanisms offered by each advertising partner listed below.

10. Advertising formats and user controls

Each advertising format in the App is described below, together with the reason it is displayed and the controls available to you.

  • App-open (splash) advertising. A full-screen placement shown when you open the App. It is displayed to sustain the free availability of the App and, where consent has been given, may be selected using your advertising identifier.
  • Rewarded video advertising. A video placement that the user chooses to watch in exchange for a reward, such as a promotional credit or a feature unlock. The reward is granted only after the video has been watched to completion; if you close the video before it finishes, no reward is granted.
  • Interstitial advertising. A full-screen placement shown at a natural break between sections of the App, such as on returning from a completed order. Frequency capping is applied so that placements do not repeat in quick succession.
  • Banner advertising. A static or rotating strip anchored to the edge of a screen. It is the least intrusive format and is generally displayed alongside functional content rather than over it.

The following controls are available to you:

  • Rewarded video opt-out. Where the App offers it, a setting allows you to stop being offered rewarded video placements.
  • Frequency capping. Ad delivery is subject to per-format caps, which limit how often a given format is shown to you.
  • Ad-free purchase. Where the App offers it, an in-app purchase removes advertising for the period purchased, subject to the store's refund policy as described in our Terms of Service.
  • Operating system controls. Limit Ad Tracking on iOS and the deletion of the advertising ID and ad personalisation opt-out on Android apply across the device, including to this App.
  • Personalised and contextual advertising. Personalised advertising uses your advertising identifier and, with consent, activity data and inferred interests to select ads addressed to you. Contextual advertising is selected from the content of the page or the context of the screen, does not require your advertising identifier, and is served where consent has been refused or withdrawn.

15. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

The Do Not Track (DNT) signal is a browser setting that has not been adopted as an industry standard. We do not currently treat a DNT signal as a request to opt out of personalised advertising or to limit data processing, and we do not interpret the signal as creating a legal right to object.

We do honour the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal where it is a legally recognised opt-out. When we receive a valid GPC signal from a browser on a supported domain, we treat it as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we pass the signal to our advertising partners. Where the state privacy laws in section 4 provide for universal opt-out mechanisms, we apply the opt-out to the same activities and to the same scope of data.

In regions that have no recognised signal or standard, we rely on the consent banner or in-app consent prompt described in section 9, and on the controls described in sections 10 and 11, which allow an equivalent choice to be made. You may also opt out by writing to support@yonghengzx.com.
